Where State Lives

A small Razor Pages app saves the word blue seven different ways. Do something a user would do, then see which of the seven the server can still read on the next request, and why.

Then do one of these

A good order to try them in
  1. Save, then click a plain link. Then click the link again.
  2. Save, submit the form, and submit it again.
  3. Save, then post and get redirected. Refresh.
  4. Save, then open a new tab. Visit from a different browser.
  5. Save, then close and reopen the browser.
  6. Save, then restart the app.

What happened: Nothing yet. The browser has loaded /State and nothing is saved.

Every result here was checked against a real ASP.NET Core 10 app driven by a real Chromium browser. Two things depend on the browser and the server. Some browsers have a "continue where you left off" setting that brings back session cookies after a restart, so Session and TempData could survive there. TempData also survived the app restart here because the app's encryption keys were saved on disk, which is the default on your own computer and in Codespaces.
